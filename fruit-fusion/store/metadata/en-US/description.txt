A glass jar, a pile of fruit and one simple rule: two identical fruits that touch fuse into the next, bigger fruit.

Drag to aim, let go to drop. Blueberries become sour cherries, cherries become apricots, and step by step you climb all the way up to the dragon fruit. Every drop moves the whole pile, so a single well-placed fruit can set off a chain of fusions that rolls through the jar.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag left or right to aim, release to drop.
• Match two identical fruits to fuse them into the next size.
• Keep the pile below the line at the rim. If fruit stays above it for too long, the round is over.
• Peek at the next fruit and plan your move.
