# Age rating questionnaire — v1.0

Expected result: **4+** (lowest global tier). Apple's current global tiers are 4+, 9+, 13+, 16+, 18+ (since the
2025 questionnaire update). The questionnaire wording and tiers change from time to time — **confirm the exact questions
and the generated rating in App Store Connect → App Information → Age Rating**.

Content facts the answers are based on: cartoon fruit that merges and pops; no characters, no faces, no conflict, no
text chat, no web views, no user-generated content, no links out, no ads, no purchases, no gambling or randomised paid
rewards. Next-fruit selection uses a seeded RNG but nothing is wagered or bought.
