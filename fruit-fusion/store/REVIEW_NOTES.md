## Notes (paste into "Notes" — English, App Review works in English)

```
Thank you for reviewing this app.

- Offline single-player puzzle game. No login, no account, no network access, no ads, no in-app purchases.
- How to play: drag horizontally to aim, release to drop a fruit. Two identical fruits that touch merge into the next,
  larger fruit. The round ends when fruit stays above the line near the top of the jar for about 2.5 seconds.
- A 3-step tutorial appears on first launch. "How to play" is also available from the main menu.
- Settings: sound, music, haptics, language (Turkish / English). The app follows the device language on first
  launch; it can be switched in Settings.
- Leaving the game saves the current run locally; "Continue" on the main menu resumes it.
- All data (best score, settings, current run) is stored only on the device. Nothing is collected or transmitted.
- All artwork, audio, fonts (SIL Open Font License) and names are original or properly licensed; the art and sound
  are generated by our own scripts.
```
