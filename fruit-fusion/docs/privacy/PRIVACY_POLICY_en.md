This privacy policy explains how the mobile game **<<APP NAME>>** (the "App") handles information. The App is
provided by **<<DEVELOPER / LEGAL ENTITY NAME>>**, <<ADDRESS>> ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the
purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698
(KVKK), to the extent either applies.

## Summary

**The App does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.** It works fully offline, has no
user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

## 1. Information stored on your device only

To make the game work, the App saves the following on your device, inside the App's private storage area:

- your best score,
- your settings (sound, music, haptics, language, whether the tutorial has been shown),
- the state of an unfinished game so you can continue later.

This information never leaves your device through the App and we cannot access it. It is not linked to your identity.
You can delete it at any time by deleting the App. If you use iCloud or computer backups, your device's operating
system may include this App data in your backups; that is governed by your backup settings and Apple's privacy policy,
not by us.

## 2. Information we collect

None. The App does not connect to the internet, and it does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera,
microphone, location or any other personal information. It does not use advertising identifiers and does not track
you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. Information collected by Apple

When you download the App from the App Store, Apple processes data (for example your Apple Account and purchase
history) under Apple's own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share crash reports and usage statistics with app
developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymised statistics and crash reports.
We use these only to fix problems and improve the App.

## 4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect
personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, e.g. 16 under GDPR in some EU countries).
If you believe a child has sent us personal data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

## 5. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and whatever you write to us. We use this only to
answer you, keep it no longer than necessary to handle your request (at most <<RETENTION, e.g. 12 months>>), and do not
share it with others except our email service provider. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to
your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK (Article 11) you have the right to ask whether we process your personal data, to access,
correct or delete it, to object to processing, to learn to whom it was transferred, and to lodge a complaint with a
supervisory authority (for Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu; in the EU: your local data protection authority).
Because the App does not collect personal data, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete beyond any email
correspondence. To exercise your rights, write to <<CONTACT EMAIL>>.
