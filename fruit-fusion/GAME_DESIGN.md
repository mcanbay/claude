# Fruit Fusion — Game Design

## Pitch
Drop fruit into a glass jar. Two identical fruits that touch fuse into the next, bigger fruit.
Plan chain reactions, keep the pile under the rim, chase your best score. One-thumb, portrait, offline.

## Originality
All names, art, audio, UI and branding are original and procedurally generated by scripts in `tools/`.
Own fruit ladder (11 tiers, Anatolian/Mediterranean set), own jar-shaped play field, own "Fusion Burst"
top-tier rule, no mascot faces. No assets, names or layout taken from existing games.
NOTE (human decision): "Fruit Fusion" may already be taken as an App Store name — check availability; fallback names in `store/`.

## Rules
- Drag horizontally to aim; release (or tap) to drop. 0.5 s cooldown between drops. "Next" fruit is shown.
- Chain combo: merges within 1.0 s of the previous merge raise the combo; multiplier = 1 + 0.5·(combo−1), capped ×3.
- Danger line near the jar rim. A landed fruit whose top is above the line counts as "over".
  If any fruit is over continuously for 2.5 s → game over. Warning (pulse + sound + haptic) while counting.
- Restart is immediate. Best score saved locally. Leaving/pausing saves the run; menu offers "Continue".

## Deterministic merge resolution
Each physics tick the game collects touching same-tier pairs, then `MergeResolver` (pure):
1. normalises every pair to (low uid, high uid) and de-duplicates,
2. sorts by tier desc, then low uid, then high uid,
3. greedily accepts pairs whose two fruits are not yet consumed this tick.
Accepted fruits are flagged `consumed` immediately and removed; exactly one new fruit is spawned per pair at the
midpoint with averaged velocity. New fruit grows its collider from 70 % to 100 % over 0.12 s to avoid explosive pushes.
uids are monotonically increasing integers → order is independent of scene-tree or signal order.

## Screens
Main menu (Play / Continue / Settings / How to play, best score) · HUD (score, best, next, pause) ·
Pause · Settings (sound, music, haptics, language) · Tutorial (3 steps, shown on first run) · Game over (score, new best, replay, menu).

## Feel
Squash-pop on merge, particle burst in fruit colour, floating score text, combo label, rim pulse in danger,
merge sound pitched by tier, light/medium/heavy haptics. Soft original music loop.

## Tech
Base viewport 720×1280, `canvas_items` stretch, `expand` aspect; layout computed from viewport + safe area.
60 Hz physics with interpolation. Future rewarded ads through the `AdService` autoload (stub in v1).
